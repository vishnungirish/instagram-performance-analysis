## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyses Instagram performance data to understand how content performs across key social media metrics and identify patterns that can support better content decisions.

The analysis focuses on translating raw Instagram performance data into meaningful KPIs, visualisations and actionable insights through an interactive Tableau dashboard.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis was designed to answer questions such as:

- How is the account performing overall?
- Which posts and content types generate the strongest engagement?
- How does engagement change over time?
- Which factors appear to influence post performance?
- Are there identifiable patterns between reach, impressions and engagement?
- What opportunities exist to improve future content strategy?

## 🔍 Analysis

The analysis examined Instagram performance across 50,000 posts to understand overall account performance and identify the factors associated with stronger audience engagement.

The analysis focused on five key areas:

- Overall account performance and engagement
- Posting frequency and engagement by weekday
- Top-performing posts by likes and engagement rate
- Performance across content formats and hashtags
- The relationship between visibility and deeper audience interactions

Rather than evaluating success using likes alone, the analysis considered reach, comments, shares, saves and engagement rate to provide a broader view of content performance.

## 💡 Key Insights

### 1. Strong overall visibility and engagement

Across the analysed dataset, the account generated:

- **987.4M total reach**
- **147.7M likes**
- **18.5M comments**
- **18.54% average engagement rate**

These metrics indicate substantial audience visibility and interaction across the analysed period.

### 2. Posting day had limited influence on engagement

Posting activity remained relatively consistent, with full months generally recording approximately **4,000–4,300 posts**.

Wednesday generated the highest average engagement rate at **18.63%**, followed closely by Saturday at **18.60%**, while Sunday recorded the lowest at **18.42%**.

However, the difference between the highest and lowest days was only **0.21 percentage points**, suggesting that content quality and campaign characteristics may be more important than posting day alone.

### 3. The most-liked posts were not necessarily the most engaging

Post **27398** generated the highest number of likes at **11,392**, while Post **23785** achieved the highest engagement rate at **34.10%**.

The strongest posts by engagement achieved approximately **33.8%–34.1% engagement**, considerably above the overall dataset average of **18.54%**.

This demonstrates why content performance should be evaluated using multiple metrics rather than likes alone.

### 4. No single content format dominated performance

Stories recorded the highest average likes at approximately **2,964** and marginally led average comments at approximately **371**.

However, Images, Reels, Stories and Videos performed relatively similarly overall, indicating that no single content format provided a substantial performance advantage.

### 5. Hashtag performance was also relatively consistent

**#BoldLooks** achieved the highest average reach at approximately **19.86K**, with **#CrueltyFree** performing extremely closely and also appearing as the most frequently used hashtag.

Overall reach differences between the analysed hashtags were small, suggesting that hashtag selection alone was unlikely to be a major performance driver.

### 6. Strong visibility did not translate equally into deeper engagement

Content generated approximately **1.28B impressions** and **987.4M aggregate reach**, with likes representing the dominant form of active engagement at **147.7M**.

Deeper interactions were substantially lower:

- **18.5M comments**
- **8.12M shares**
- **8.86M saves**

This reveals an opportunity to move audiences beyond exposure and lightweight interaction toward behaviours such as commenting, sharing and saving content.

## 💼 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

- **Maintain posting consistency** rather than significantly increasing content volume.
- Use **Wednesday and Saturday for priority content testing**, while recognising that weekday differences are small.
- Maintain a **mixed-format strategy** across Images, Reels, Stories and Videos rather than relying heavily on one format.
- Evaluate performance using **engagement rate, comments, shares and saves alongside likes**.
- Analyse the characteristics of posts achieving approximately **34% engagement** to identify repeatable creative patterns.
- Use stronger **calls-to-action, questions, polls and participation-driven content** to encourage deeper interaction.
- Create more **saveable content**, including tutorials, routines and product guides.
- Continue using a diverse hashtag strategy while further testing **#BoldLooks** and **#CrueltyFree**.
- Test creative concepts, campaign types and messaging rather than assuming posting day, format or hashtag alone drives performance.

### Future Analysis

Additional data could strengthen future analysis by incorporating:

- Posting time to identify optimal hours
- Follower growth over time
- Campaign objectives and advertising spend
- Caption and call-to-action characteristics
- A/B testing to determine whether observed differences translate into meaningful performance improvements
